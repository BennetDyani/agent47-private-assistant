# Malume recording script: Setswana

Record **one clip per line**. Name each clip with its code (e.g. `tn01`). Speak naturally, the way you would talk to a real person.
If you say something different from the text, that's fine: just tell us what you actually said.

- **tn01**: Dumela Malome, o tsogile jang?
- **tn02**: Ke kopa o mpolelele gore ke nako mang.
- **tn03**: Maemo a bosa a tla nna jang kamoso kwa Gauteng?
- **tn04**: Nkgopotse go leletsa mme ka ura ya borataro maitseboa.
- **tn05**: Ke kopa o mpalele di-imeile tsa me tse dintšhwa.
- **tn06**: Kwalela Thabo molaetsa o re ke tla fitlha morago ga nako.
- **tn07**: Ke kopa o ntlhalosetse gore ke eng se se mo sekirining sa me.
- **tn08**: Ke a leboga thata Malome, o nthusitse.
- **tn09**: Ke batla go apaya dijo tsa maitseboa, nka apaya eng?
- **tn10**: A pula e tla na gompieno motshegare?
- **tn11**: Kopano ya me e ka la 15 Ngwanatsele.
- **tn12**: Ke kopa o leletse Kagiso Molefe.
- **tn13** _(mix)_: Malome, please check di-emails tsa me, a go na le sengwe se se tswang kwa bank?
- **tn14** _(mix)_: Ke kopa o set-e alarm ya six o'clock kamoso mo mosong.
- **tn15** _(mix)_: Eish, load shedding e tla simolola leng gompieno?
- **tn16** _(mix)_: Bula Spotify o tshameke music ya Black Coffee.
- **tn17** _(mix)_: Nthuse go kwala email go boss wa me, ke re ke tla tla late.
- **tn18** _(mix)_: Ke eng se se mo screen sa me? Ke kopa o explain-e.
- **tn19** _(mix)_: Meeting ya rona e santse e le ka Friday, ne?
- **tn20** _(mix)_: Ke kopa o check-e gore weather e tla nna jang this weekend.
- **tn21** _(free speech)_: (Tell Malome about your day in Setswana, mixing English if you like. About 10 seconds.)
- **tn22** _(free speech)_: (Ask Malome for help with anything, in your own words. About 10 seconds.)
