# Malume recording script: isiZulu

Record **one clip per line**. Name each clip with its code (e.g. `zu01`). Speak naturally, the way you would talk to a real person.
If you say something different from the text, that's fine: just tell us what you actually said.

- **zu01**: Sawubona Malume, unjani namuhla?
- **zu02**: Ngicela ungitshele ukuthi sithini isikhathi.
- **zu03**: Isimo sezulu sizoba njani kusasa eGoli?
- **zu04**: Ngikhumbuze ukushayela umama ngehora lesithupha kusihlwa.
- **zu05**: Ngicela ungifundele ama-imeyili ami amasha.
- **zu06**: Bhalela uThabo umlayezo uthi ngizofika sekwephuzile.
- **zu07**: Ngicela ungichazele ukuthi yini esesikrinini sami.
- **zu08**: Ngiyabonga kakhulu Malume, ungisizile.
- **zu09**: Ngifuna ukupheka isidlo sakusihlwa, ngingapheka ini?
- **zu10**: Ingabe izulu lizokuna namuhla ntambama?
- **zu11**: Umhlangano wami ungomhlaka-15 kuLwezi.
- **zu12**: Ngicela ushayele uSipho Dlamini.
- **zu13** _(mix)_: Malume, please check ama-emails ami, ngabe kukhona okuvela ebhange?
- **zu14** _(mix)_: Ngicela u-set-e i-alarm ye-six o'clock kusasa ekuseni.
- **zu15** _(mix)_: Eish, i-load shedding izoqala nini namuhla?
- **zu16** _(mix)_: Vula i-Spotify bese u-play-a i-music ka-Black Coffee.
- **zu17** _(mix)_: Ngisize ngi-write-e i-email eya ku-boss wami, ngithi ngizophuza.
- **zu18** _(mix)_: Yini le esesikrinini sami? Ngicela u-explain-e kancane.
- **zu19** _(mix)_: I-meeting yethu isekhona yini on Friday?
- **zu20** _(mix)_: Ngicela u-check-e ukuthi i-weather izoba njani this weekend.
- **zu21** _(free speech)_: (Tell Malume about your day in isiZulu, mixing English if you like. About 10 seconds.)
- **zu22** _(free speech)_: (Ask Malume for help with anything, in your own words. About 10 seconds.)
