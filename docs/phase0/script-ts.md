# Malume recording script: Xitsonga

Record **one clip per line**. Name each clip with its code (e.g. `ts01`). Speak naturally, the way you would talk to a real person.
If you say something different from the text, that's fine: just tell us what you actually said.

- **ts01**: Avuxeni Malume, u njhani namuntlha?
- **ts02**: Ndzi kombela u ndzi byela leswaku i nkarhi muni.
- **ts03**: Maxelo ma ta va njhani mundzuku eGauteng?
- **ts04**: Ndzi tsundzuxe ku ringela manana nimadyambu.
- **ts05**: Ndzi kombela u ndzi hlayela ti-imeyili ta mina letintshwa.
- **ts06**: Tsalela Thabo rungula u ku ndzi ta hlwela.
- **ts07**: Ndzi kombela u ndzi hlamusela leswi nga eka xikirini xa mina.
- **ts08**: Ndza khensa swinene Malume, u ndzi pfunile.
- **ts09**: Xana mpfula yi ta na namuntlha nimadyambu?
- **ts10**: Ndzi kombela u ringela Tsakani Baloyi.
- **ts11** _(mix)_: Malume, please check ti-email ta mina, xana ku na nchumu lowu humaka eka bank?
- **ts12** _(mix)_: Ndzi kombela u set-a alarm ya six o'clock mundzuku nimixo.
- **ts13** _(mix)_: Eish, load shedding yi ta sungula rini namuntlha?
- **ts14** _(mix)_: Pfula Spotify u tlanga music ya Black Coffee.
- **ts15** _(mix)_: Ndzi pfuna ku tsala email eka boss wa mina.
- **ts16** _(mix)_: Meeting ya hina ya ha ri hi Friday, a hi swona?
- **ts17** _(free speech)_: (Tell Malume about your day in Xitsonga, mixing English if you like. About 10 seconds.)
- **ts18** _(free speech)_: (Ask Malume for help with anything, in your own words. About 10 seconds.)
